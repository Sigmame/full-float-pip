# Curated recent Asian YouTube films

The popup offers 15 live-action films released in 2021–2024, prioritizing Hong
Kong films and films with Hong Kong action stars. Mainland productions with
Hong Kong actors retain their actual region; the filter is explicitly labelled
“Hồng Kông & sao HK”. AI narration, dubbing and translated subtitles are allowed.

## Selection policy

Use publisher channels (iQIYI, YOUKU, Tencent/WeTV). Check the original title,
cast, synopsis and feature-length runtime against the film identity. Exclude
generative AI films, misleading identities, trailers, previews, recaps, scene
compilations and unrelated uploads. Keep original release years independently
of upload dates and promotional titles such as “Chinese movie 2026”. Vietnamese
alternate names are browsing labels; the original title is searchable too.

This is a manually selected allowlist, not an automated detector for every
YouTube video. Metadata cannot prove every frame matches the film or detect
all undisclosed AI generation. The selection was checked against publisher
metadata and feature-length runtimes on 2026-09-30. All 15 YouTube player responses
reported OK and playableInEmbed=true from this machine; regional restrictions,
account requirements and later removals can still affect playback. Original
multi-subtitle tracks are labelled as such rather than promising Vietnamese.

No YouTube API key or extra browser host permission is required. Only selected
IDs can play in the native preferred embed path. Removed IDs no longer qualify,
including when retained in history. The list does not load players until the
user selects a movie. Direct YouTube links remain available.

## Updating and verification

Update `extension/site-access.js` and `FloatWindow.curatedYouTubeMovieIDs`
together. `extension/tests/popup-hot.test.js` checks identical native/extension
allowlists, retired-ID rejection, filters and original-title/cast search.
`site-access.test.js` checks browsing and mismatched embed denial.

`native-app/tests/youtube-player.test.js` checks progress, pause/play, seek,
volume, mute and error reporting. `native-app/tests/youtube-playback-e2e.py`
defaults to Phá chiến and requires the playback clock to advance with a valid
feature duration. Use `--binary` to check an installed companion. Rebuild the
native companion after changing the allowlist; reload the unpacked extension.
